      Interview questions
      
                
      Both sides of the conversation. Questions to ask them prepares the moment the interviewer says “do you have any questions for us?”. Questions they may ask you predicts what you will be asked and drafts an answer you can edit into your own words. Both are written from the vacancy, what is already known about the company and your CV; the answers also draw on your saved STAR stories. Whatever you generate is saved with the vacancy, so it is there when you come back, and both halves download as a Word or text file you can edit. This is not about your documents: Document Review checks those.
      
                You ask the employer
      
                
      
                    
                    
                
      
                
      Select a single user to prepare interview questions.
      
                
      
                
      
                    
      
                        
      1. Set the conversation
      
                        
                        
      One setup for both halves: whichever you generate uses the vacancy, language and CV chosen here. Choosing a vacancy shows what you saved for it.
      
                        
                        
                        
                        
                        
      Your own CV file, LinkedIn import and profile are always used. A CV Builder profile is optional: Automatic uses every source you have. Save any CV Builder changes first.
      
                        
                        
                        
      Your facts, the vacancy, what web search found about the company and your notes go to your configured language model. If the company has not been looked up yet, that happens first and adds some time. Company facts come only from web search results; the model is told not to add any of its own.
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                        
      
                    
      
                    
      
                        
      
                            
      2. Take these into the room
      
                            
      Your questions will appear here, grouped by theme, each one with why it matters for you and what it was based on.
      
                            
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                
      
                                
      A model wrote these from your CV and this vacancy. Read them before you use them, and drop any that no longer fit the conversation. The Word file opens in Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs, and every question in it can be rewritten or deleted.
      
                            
      
                        
      
                        
      
                            
      2. Rehearse your answers
      
                            
      The questions this interviewer is likely to ask will appear here, grouped by what they are probing for, each with why it is likely to come up and a draft answer you can edit. Anything marked as a gap is the one to rehearse.
      
                            
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                
      
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                
      
                                
      These are drafts, not lines to learn: rewrite each one until it sounds like you. Your edits are saved with this vacancy as you type, and both downloads and Copy all take the text as you edited it. Generating again replaces the saved answers in that language, edits included.

      What do the progress choices mean?
      To review
      You have not decided what to do with this vacancy.
      Interested
      You want to pursue it or prepare an application.
      Applied
      You have sent your application.
      Interviewing
      You are in contact about an interview or assessment.
      Offer received
      The employer has made an offer.
      Closed
      You are no longer pursuing it, or the vacancy is no longer available.
      You can choose any step directly and change it later. Pinning is a separate bookmark. Neither action sends an application.

      Import from LinkedIn
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                        
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                            
      
                                On your LinkedIn profile choose More → Save to PDF,
                                then give the downloaded file's path here. This is the only
                                import that reliably recovers your full job history with
                                dates, so it can also fix an out-of-date current role.
                            
      
                        
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                        
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                        
      
                        
      
                            
                            
                            
      
                                Note: LinkedIn hides work history from automated requests,
                                so this returns only education and public skills. Use paste
                                or export for full work experience.
